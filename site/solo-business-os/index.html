The details

Frequently asked questions.

Where is my data stored?

In the local storage of the browser profile you use to open Solo. Your business records are not uploaded to a Solo cloud account.

What happens if I clear browser data?

Your workspace may be erased. Export backups regularly and keep a recent copy somewhere safe.

Can I move to another computer?

Yes. Export a JSON backup on the original computer, open Solo on the new one, then import that backup. Importing replaces the workspace currently open there. There is no automatic sync.

Can my team use it together?

This edition is designed for one owner. It does not provide live multi-user collaboration.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The product itself opens locally in a modern browser without an account or internet connection. Purchasing and downloading it require access to the sales channel.

Is my data secure?

Your business records stay in your browser profile rather than a Solo-hosted database. That does not replace device security or backups: anyone with access to your unlocked device or backup files may be able to access your records.

Does it send invoices, collect payments or handle taxes?

No. Money is for internal operational tracking of income and expenses. Continue using appropriate invoicing, payment and accounting tools where needed.
